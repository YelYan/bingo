## Pain points, in their words (paraphrased)
1. **Price is the first question.** "How much monthly vs lifetime?" "Cost-friendly please." Skincare and clothing owners ask for monthly fee vs one-off price including the machine.
2. **Stock with thousands of items.** Owners with 400 to several thousand items want Excel import so they do not type each item (stock in/out must be exact).
3. **Retail and wholesale prices in one system.** Clothing, grocery and rice shops sell the same item at several prices or pack sizes (rice 100 lb, 50 lb, 25 lb bags).
4. **Phone-based use.** Pharmacy wants a mostly-phone POS, price change that is easy, and printing A5 vouchers straight from a phone to an inkjet over wifi. Clothing shop uses tablet + Bluetooth printer.
5. **Printers.** Thermal receipt printers (2 inch) and customer displays come up; people ask where to buy or resell used ones. Hardware is a real cost and source of confusion.
6. **Trust in vendors.** A rice-shop owner paid a deposit to a named software company and then it "did not work out". People ask for real user feedback on a product before buying.
7. **Too many options.** Posts ask "which one is easiest and most used". They do not know how to compare.
8. **Reports.** Daily sales, expense and accounting reports are named as needed.
9. **Barcode and Excel/ERP.** Some answers push Odoo ERP; owners with simple needs do not want ERP.

## Who is answering (competitors)
Vendors reply under almost every post with a product pitch and a phone number: Info Tech Computer (Easy Stock Pro), OAxis365 Cloud POS (by XYPHA TECH), Gold Grade Software Studio, NovaBiz Myanmar, ACE System, Odoo resellers, and others. Strong signal that this is a crowded, competitive market with small price-sensitive buyers.

## What this means for BingoMart
- These buyers are **offline shops**, not online-only. BingoMart's online-shop pitch only fits those who also sell on Facebook or TikTok.
- Strong overlap with our first-feature idea: **stock log, daily revenue, simple POS from a phone, Excel import**.
- Price transparency (monthly price shown upfront) and a free trial would answer the first and sixth pain points.
- Do not post sales pitches; owners here ask for recommendations. The survey is closer to a poll than an advert, but vendor-heavy comment threads show admins may remove promotion.
